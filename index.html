About Me

I'm a Data Engineer at Cliff IT Solutions. Since April 2025, I have been developing Azure-based ETL pipelines as part of a legacy-to-cloud data replatforming initiative: migrating transformation workflows from Teradata, Informatica and Control-M to Azure, writing PySpark, Spark SQL and Python in Azure Databricks notebooks, and building pipelines in Azure Data Factory within an established solution architecture.

AI & Research Interests

Building data pipelines has shown me how much any downstream system, including an AI system, depends on the data and processes beneath it. I want to study these questions more rigorously, building on my M.Tech coursework and independent reading.

  • Technical foundations of AI

    How modern machine learning systems work, from the data they learn from to the behaviour they produce.

  • Reliability and evaluation

    How AI systems are tested and measured, and how we can tell when their outputs can be relied on.

  • Data quality and trustworthy AI

    How missing, stale or skewed data carries through a pipeline and into what a model learns.

  • Responsible AI development

    The practices and governance that help AI systems be developed safely and accountably.

  • Machine learning and predictive modelling

    Deepening the introductory experience I gained through internships and coursework.

My professional experience is in data engineering. My machine learning experience so far is academic and introductory, and I am at the start of exploring AI research.
